About the Competition

Welcome to Hack The Intelligence

Hack The Intelligence is a national innovation competition hosted as part of the AISEC International Summit 2026 — the premier international conference on Artificial Intelligence and Cybersecurity.

Participants are encouraged to register, develop their projects in advance, and pitch their innovative ideas in front of a panel of experts and professionals from industry and academia.

Whether you're working on AI-powered security solutions, smart city innovations, IoT systems, or cybersecurity tools — this competition is your chance to turn ideas into impactful solutions.
