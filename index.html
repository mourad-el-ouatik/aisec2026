About the Event

What is AiSec?

AISEC 2026 — Official Teaser Video

AiSec is the leading international summit dedicated to Artificial Intelligence and Cybersecurity, held annually at ENSA National School of Applied Science, Cadi Ayad University, Marrakech, Morocco.

The summit brings together world-class researchers, industry leaders, government officials, and students from Africa, the Arab World, and beyond to explore the intersection of AI and cyber threats.

AiSec features a Scientific Conference with peer-reviewed papers, an international CTF Competition, an innovative Hackathon Competition, expert keynotes, hands-on workshops, and high-level networking sessions.

Competitions

Hack The Intelligence – National Innovation Challenge

An intensive innovation competition where participants design and build cutting-edge solutions at the intersection of Artificial Intelligence and Cybersecurity. Part of the AISEC International Summit 2026.

  • Open to students, developers, and innovators across Morocco
  • Four competition tracks: Cybersecurity, Artificial Intelligence, IoT & Smart Cities
  • Real-world problem statements from industry and academia
  • Team-based collaboration and rapid prototyping
  • Pitch your solution in front of experts and win recognition & prizes

Join the Competition →

CTF Competition

A dynamic Capture The Flag competition where participants solve cybersecurity challenges, enhanced with AI-driven scenarios and modern attack simulations.

  • Categories: Web, Crypto, Reverse, Forensics, PWN
  • AI-enhanced challenges and threat simulations
  • Real-time scoring and leaderboard
  • On-site finals for top teams
  • Cash prizes, certificates, and networking opportunities
