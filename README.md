## ◈ About the Event

> *AiSec is an annual international summit at the intersection of Artificial Intelligence and Cybersecurity — hosted at the National School of Applied Science (ENSA), Cadi Ayad University, Marrakech, Morocco.*

The summit unites **world-class researchers**, **industry leaders**, **government officials**, and **students** from Africa, the Arab World, and beyond — for three days of keynotes, workshops, research papers, and live CTF competition.
